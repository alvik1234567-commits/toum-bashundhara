## Why it's worded this way
- **"Free Claude"** means Claude's free plan (claude.ai / app), which has daily usage limits. There's no free paid subscription, so the script doesn't promise one.
- **No income numbers or "get rich quick" claims.** Facebook can limit pages that make income promises, and they mislead viewers. The footer disclaimer protects the page.
- Claude can't edit video or make images itself, so the video-editing and ads points say what it really helps with (scripts, subtitles, ad copy, ideas), with the editing done in CapCut.
- The comment keyword (“ক্লড”) boosts engagement, and you can reply with the detailed guide.
- Each way is something a beginner in Bangladesh can actually start with, with Claude as a helper and the person still doing and checking the work.

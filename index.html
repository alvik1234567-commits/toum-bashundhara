Why “Toum”?

It's the garlic.
It was always the garlic.

In Lebanon, toum is the snow-white garlic sauce that turns a good shawarma into the one you think about all week. Garlic, lemon, oil — whipped until it's fluffy as a cloud and sharp enough to wake you up.

We named the restaurant after it because everything else on the menu is built around it: chicken roasted on the spit, tawouk and adana kebab over real charcoal, rice cooked Arabian-style with long-grain basmati.
